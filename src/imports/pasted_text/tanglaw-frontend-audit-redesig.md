# Tanglaw Branding Context

This platform is **Tanglaw P2P**, an offline-first AI-powered community verification platform built specifically for the Philippines. The visual identity should reflect its mission of **trust, resilience, accessibility, community empowerment, and media literacy**.

The Light Mode should evoke a sense of **hope, clarity, safety, and guidance**—living up to the meaning of *Tanglaw* ("light" or "torch"). Draw inspiration from sunrise tones, clean civic technology interfaces, and modern government digital services, while maintaining a premium SaaS aesthetic. Every design decision should reinforce trust and make the application approachable for diverse users, including students, barangay officials, educators, NGOs, humanitarian organizations, senior citizens, and first-time smartphone users.
