### Typography Color System

Establish a consistent typography hierarchy for Light Mode.

Suggested colors:

**Primary Text**

* Dark Navy (#0F172A or similar)
* Nearly Black

**Secondary Text**

* Dark Gray

**Body Text**

* Medium Dark Gray

**Supporting Text**

* Slate Gray

**Muted Text**

* Medium Gray

**Disabled Text**

* Light Gray with sufficient contrast

**Highlighted Text**

* Navy
* Red (only when emphasizing important content)
* Gold only when used on sufficiently dark backgrounds

Avoid:

* White text on light backgrounds
* Light gray text on cream backgrounds
* Gold text on warm backgrounds
* Low-contrast combinations
* Transparent text that reduces readability

Every typography choice should satisfy **WCAG AA**, with WCAG AAA preferred whenever possible.
