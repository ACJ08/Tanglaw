# Prompt

Act as a **Senior Frontend Engineer, Senior UI/UX Designer, Visual Designer, Design Systems Architect, Product Designer, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) Specialist, Accessibility Expert (WCAG AA/AAA), Motion Designer, and CSS/Tailwind Expert** with over **15 years of experience** designing award-winning SaaS platforms, AI applications, government systems, civic technology platforms, and enterprise dashboards.

Your task is to **perform a complete frontend design review and redesign** of the entire Tanglaw P2P platform. Before making any changes, **analyze every frontend file, component, page, layout, reusable UI element, CSS/Tailwind styles, theme configuration, animations, typography, spacing, and color system** to ensure every design decision contributes to a cohesive and professional user experience.

Do **not** simply change colors. Think like a professional product designer creating a polished, production-ready design system. Every page should feel visually connected, modern, elegant, and consistent.

The final Light Mode should look comparable to high-quality products such as **Microsoft, Google Material 3, Apple, Stripe, Notion, Linear, Figma, Vercel, and Adobe**, while preserving Tanglaw's unique Filipino identity and mission.

# Overall Objective

The application already supports Light Mode and Dark Mode, but the **Light Mode requires a complete visual refinement**.

Your objective is to redesign Light Mode into a **premium, warm, welcoming, trustworthy, and highly readable interface** that reflects the meaning of **Tanglaw** ("light" or "torch") while maintaining excellent accessibility and usability.

The design should communicate:

* Trust
* Hope
* Safety
* Guidance
* Community
* Innovation
* Professionalism
* Accessibility

The Light Mode should have its own identity rather than being a simple inversion of Dark Mode.

# New Light Mode Color Direction

I would like the Light Mode to adopt a **warm and inviting aesthetic** inspired by sunlight and hope.

The primary background should lean toward a **soft gold or warm cream tone**, rather than plain white. This creates a brighter and more welcoming experience while reinforcing the concept of "Tanglaw" as a guiding light.

Suggested background palette:

* Warm Cream
* Soft Ivory
* Light Gold
* Champagne
* Very Light Beige

These backgrounds should remain subtle and never overpower the content.

The interface should still feel modern, clean, and spacious.

# Design Philosophy

The Light Mode should embody the spirit of **Tanglaw**—a beacon of truth, guidance, and hope for Filipino communities. The interface should feel warm, approachable, and trustworthy without sacrificing professionalism. Use the warm gold-inspired backgrounds to create a welcoming atmosphere, while Navy Blue conveys reliability and Red draws attention to critical information and meaningful highlights. Every design choice should reinforce Tanglaw's mission of empowering communities with verified information, making the platform feel polished, inclusive, accessible, and ready for real-world deployment.
